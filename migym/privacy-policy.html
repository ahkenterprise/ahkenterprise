1. Who we are

MiGym Pro ("MiGym", "we", "us", "our") is a gym management platform operated from India. We provide software to gym owners to manage memberships, track member fitness progress, control door access, sell products, and communicate with members. Members of partner gyms use MiGym Pro to access their own memberships and interact with their gym.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and your rights. It applies to the MiGym Pro Android app (the "App") and any related web interface at the same domain.

Data Controller: MiGym Pro. Contact: privacy@migympro.com

4. Biometric & face data (important)

⚠ Face ID is a convenience feature, not a security measure. MiGym's Face ID hashes a low-resolution camera frame and is trivially spoofable with a photograph. It is intended to reduce friction for members entering their gym — it is not suitable for high-security access control. For high-security use cases, we recommend the gym owner configures a proper door-lock integration (Tuya, ZKTeco/Hikvision, or HTTP API) and uses their device's own biometric verification.

What we store: When a member enrolls in Face ID, their camera frame is processed entirely on-device. We do not upload the raw image. We compute a 1024-bit binary template (a string of 1s and 0s), and store only that string in their member record. This string is not reversible into an image.

What we do NOT store: We do not store the camera frame, video, or any raw biometric image.

Where the template is used: (a) The gym's kiosk camera compares a live frame against enrolled templates to match a member; (b) The member's own phone can compare their live frame against their own template to unlock the door themselves.

Your control: You can delete your Face ID template at any time from the app's Settings → Delete Account. You can also ask the gym owner to reset your face enrollment. You may use the app without Face ID — it is opt-in.

Consent: Face enrollment requires an explicit tap on "Capture & Enroll". By tapping, you consent to the collection and use of your biometric template as described above.

India DPDP Act 2023: We treat biometric data as sensitive personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We rely on your explicit consent and provide a specific, informed notice at the point of collection. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your face template.

6. Data retention

  • Active accounts: Retained while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: Profile and biometric data deleted within 30 days. Aggregate anonymized stats (e.g., "42 members joined in March") may be kept.
  • Payment records: Kept for 7 years where required by tax law (this applies to the gym owner's financial records).
  • Crash logs: Kept for 90 days.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (most fields are editable in your profile).
  • Delete your account and associated data (see section 8).
  • Withdraw consent for Face ID or AI features without losing access to the rest of the app.
  • Port your data — email us and we'll export it as JSON.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India or your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, email privacy@migympro.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Account & data deletion

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app:

  1. Open the MiGym Pro app.
  2. Go to Settings (gym owner) or Profile (member).
  3. Tap Delete My Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

This will permanently delete:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (you will be signed out everywhere).
  • Your user profile record.
  • Your member record (if you are a member), including your face template, payment history, and order history.

If you are a gym owner, the app will first check if you have active members. If yes, you will be asked to transfer ownership or contact support — to protect the members who depend on your gym. If no, the gym record, products, plans, and settings will be removed.

Outside the app: You can also request deletion by emailing privacy@migympro.com from your registered email address. We will confirm within 30 days.

9. Children

MiGym Pro is not intended for children under 13 (or under 18 without gym-owner + parental consent). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards:

  • HTTPS/TLS for all data in transit.
  • Firebase Authentication (email + password, optional Google Sign-In).
  • Firestore Security Rules that restrict each user to their own data and their own gym.
  • Passwords hashed by Firebase (never seen by us in plaintext).
  • Face templates stored as non-reversible binary hashes.
  • Regular dependency updates.

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please email security@migympro.com.

11. International transfers

Firebase and Cloudinary operate globally. Your data may be processed in the United States, European Union, and India. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent safeguards for cross-border transfers.
